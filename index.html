You don't need to quit your job or move abroad to study the deen properly. Here's what studying with us looks like.

Your pace, your plan

You choose the pace. We build the plan around you.

Business owners, parents, university students. Most of our students have packed days, so you decide how much time your week can give. Then Ustadh Rhyad builds a study plan around it.

  1. 1

    Choose your tier

    30, 60 or 90 minutes a day, 5 days a week, on average. You decide how much, and when.

  2. 2

    Get your personalised study plan

    Ustadh Rhyad tells you which programme to start with, which system to follow and where to go next. No guessing.

  3. 3

    Change it whenever life does

    A busy month? Step down a tier. More free time? Move up. Your plan moves with you.

Questions before you join

I'm a beginner. Is this right for me?

Muslim Lighthouse is an advanced programme. If you're still building your foundations, start with Illuminate. It's built to give you exactly those foundations, and many of our students began there.

I'm really busy. Will I keep up?

That's who it's built for. Tier 1 takes around 30 minutes a day, 5 days a week, on average. That’s about 2½ hours a week. Tier 2 is around 60 minutes a day and Tier 3 around 90, if you have more time. You can spread it however suits you: a little every day, or a couple of longer sessions at the weekend. Your personalised plan tells you exactly what to do, so none of your time is wasted.

When are the live classes?

Every weekend, on Saturday and Sunday. They're always interactive, and the recorded library is there whenever suits you.

Do I need to know Arabic?

No. Lessons are taught in English and the classical texts are explained step by step. Arabic is part of the curriculum if you'd like to learn it.

I don't live in the UK. Can I join?

Of course. Our students live all over the world and everything happens online. The yearly Umrah and UK meetup are there for anyone who can make it.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime. If you cancel, you won't be billed again.
