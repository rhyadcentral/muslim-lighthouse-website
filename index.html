Questions before you join

I'm a beginner. Is this right for me?

Muslim Lighthouse is an advanced programme. If you're still building your foundations, start with Illuminate. It's built to give you exactly those foundations, and many of our students began there.

I'm really busy. Will I keep up?

That's who it's built for. Tier 1 takes around 30 minutes a day, 5 days a week, on average. Tier 2 is around 60 minutes and Tier 3 around 90 minutes, if you have more time. Your personalised plan tells you exactly what to do, so none of your time is wasted.

When are the live classes?

Every weekend, on Saturday and Sunday. They're always interactive, and the recorded library is there whenever suits you.

Do I need to know Arabic?

No. Lessons are taught in English and the classical texts are explained step by step. Arabic is part of the curriculum if you'd like to learn it.

I don't live in the UK. Can I join?

Of course. Our students live all over the world and everything happens online. The yearly Umrah and UK meetup are there for anyone who can make it.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime. If you cancel, you won't be billed again.
