Why most people never get far

You've watched the clips. So why does it still feel like you're starting from zero?

You want to know your deen properly. Not bits and pieces, not a reminder here and a reel there, but real knowledge that settles in your heart and changes how you pray, how you speak and how you treat the people you love. Then Monday comes. Work, the kids, deadlines, exams. So you save another video for later, and later never comes.

The problem isn't you. It's the method.

Knowledge was never meant to be collected in thirty-second pieces. For fourteen centuries it has passed from teacher to student, text by text, with someone guiding you and correcting you along the way. The scholars said it plainly: whoever takes books as their only teacher makes more mistakes than they get right.

Muslim Lighthouse brings that path back, and shapes it around the life you already have.

Questions

Before you join.

I'm a beginner. Is this right for me?

Muslim Lighthouse is an advanced programme. If you're still building your foundations, start with Illuminate. It's built to give you exactly those foundations, and many of our students began there.

I'm really busy. Will I keep up?

Yes, that's who it's built for. Tier 1 takes around 30 minutes a day, 5 days a week, on average. If you have more time, Tiers 2 and 3 let you go faster. Ustadh Rhyad's personalised plan tells you exactly what to do, so none of your time is wasted.

When are the live classes?

Every weekend, on Saturday and Sunday, so they fit around work and study. They're always interactive. On top of the live classes there's a huge library of recorded courses you can study whenever suits you.

Do I need to know Arabic?

No. Lessons are taught in English and the classical texts are explained step by step, so anyone can understand them. If you want to learn Arabic, it's part of the curriculum too.

Is there a space for sisters?

Yes. There's a private space for sisters and a private space for brothers, with weekly sisters-only and brothers-only calls.

What is an Ijaza?

An Ijaza is a scholarly certification with a chain of teachers that goes back to the author of the book. It's a testament to what you have studied. Ustadh tests you before you receive it, so that he's confident you could teach that content yourself one day.

How does Muslim Lighthouse AI work?

It answers your questions using only the content Ustadh Rhyad has taught, so it doesn't make things up. It's there for quick questions on the go. For anything personal, you can message Ustadh directly.

I don't live in the UK. Can I join?

Of course. Our students live all over the world and everything happens online. The in-person events, a yearly Umrah and UK meetups, are a bonus for anyone who can make it.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime. Quarterly members are billed every 3 months and yearly members once a year. If you cancel, you won't be billed again.

What happens after I join?

You get instant access to the full library and the community through the Muslim Lighthouse app. Then Ustadh Rhyad gives you your personalised study plan: where to start, which system to follow and what comes next.
